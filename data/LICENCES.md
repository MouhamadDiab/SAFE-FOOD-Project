# Data licensing and provenance (Option A)

The included CSVs (`data/raw/`, `data/sample/`) were generated synthetically for a university exercise with OFF-shaped and USDA-shaped schemas. They are **not actually downloaded Open Food Facts or USDA records**. No claim that the official databases endorsed or provided these exact fixture rows should be made.

Open Food Facts documentation and license (relevant only if later replacing fixtures with actual OFF content): https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data (ODbL).
USDA FoodData Central (relevant only if later replacing fixtures with genuine USDA records): https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/ .
FoodOn attribution: https://foodon.org/ ; the local `foodon-slim.ttl` is a small educational identifier sketch, not a complete ontology distribution.

Keep the original synthetic data and the cleaning audit for academic traceability.
